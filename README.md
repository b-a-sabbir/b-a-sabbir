## Hi there 👋
# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I'm Sabbir<br>I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Faridpur Engineering College (affiliated with the University of Dhaka), with a growing focus on machine learning and computer vision.
Right now I'm working on two research projects - a bone fracture classification system and a pen-tip tracking model using YOLO pose estimation - both aimed at a submission to ICCIT 2026. Alongside that, I'm applying for fully-funded Master's programs in Data Science and AI, particularly through Erasmus Mundus, while also looking for opportunities as a Data Scientist / ML Engineer.
I mostly work with Python, TensorFlow/Keras, Scikit-learn, YOLO (Ultralytics), OpenCV, Pandas, and NumPy, and I enjoy turning messy data into something usable - from scraping and cleaning datasets to building small tools around them. <br>You can find more of my work on my [portfolio](https://b-a-sabbir.github.io/Portfolio-website).
